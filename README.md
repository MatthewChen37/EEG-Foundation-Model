MENDR: Manifold Explainable Neural Data Representations

An EEG foundation model that produces embeddings living on a Riemannian manifold. The manifold is the space of all Symmetric Positive Definite Matrices constructed by the sample covariance matrix of time-varying BENDR features. 

(Current updates to the project are available in the manuscript on Arxiv)[https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04956].

This project was also part of my Master's thesis at Georgia Tech.
